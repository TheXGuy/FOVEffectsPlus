# FOVEffects+
**FOVEffects+** is a mod that gives you _full_ and _extensive_ control over your **FOV** and **FOV Effects**!

## What are FOV Effects?
**FOV Effects** are modifiers added to your FOV when _specific_ actions are taken.

The current list of all supported FOV Effects are:
- ☄️ Sprinting ☄️
- ⚗️ Speed Potion Effect ⚗️
- ⚗️ Slowness Potion Effect ⚗️
- 🌟 Flying 🌟
- 🏹 Bow Aiming 🏹
- ❄️ Powdered Snow ❄️
- 👣 Soul Speed 👣
- 🌊 Submerged in Water/Lava 🌊

## How do I use the mod?
To **use the mod**, simply open the _Mods_ menu, find the _FOVEffects+_ mod, **_hover_** over the icon, and **click** to change the config.
